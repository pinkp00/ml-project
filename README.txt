Project: EnergySense AI - Hotel Energy Demand Prediction

OBJECTIVE:
To predict the electrical energy demand of a hotel based on occupancy, 
temperature, and operational hours to optimize costs.

TECH STACK:
- Python, Streamlit
- XGBoost, Random Forest, Logistic Regression
- Plotly for Interactive Visualizations

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Run: pip install -r requirements.txt
2. Execution: streamlit run app.py

INPUTS: Temperature, Occupancy, AC Hours, Kitchen Usage.
OUTPUTS: Predicted kW demand, Performance Rings, and Development Roadmap.
